SafeBand tells your family within seconds when an elder falls.

A low-cost wrist band for elders in rural India. It detects a fall, sends the location to WhatsApp, and lets the wearer cancel a false alarm with one press.

How it works

  1. The motion sensor spots a hard impact, then stillness.
  2. The band vibrates for 10 seconds. One press cancels a false alarm.
  3. No cancel: the band captures heart rate, oxygen and location.
  4. A WhatsApp message with a map link reaches the caregiver.
  5. The caregiver acknowledges it here on the dashboard.

Location uses GPS first. If GPS has no fix, it falls back to Wi-Fi, then internet location, then the last known position. The dashboard always shows which one was used.
