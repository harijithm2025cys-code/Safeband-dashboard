SafeBand tells your family within seconds when an elder falls.

A wrist-worn band that watches for falls, low oxygen, and abnormal heart rate — and alerts a caregiver over WhatsApp within seconds. Built for elders in rural India.

How it works

01 · SENSEMPU-6050 accelerometer samples 100 Hz to detect a free fall and impact.
02 · CONFIRMStillness after impact starts a 10-second countdown with a buzzer and vibration.
03 · CANCELA single press on the band's button cancels a false alarm.
04 · SENDIf not cancelled, the ESP32 posts the alert and vitals over Wi-Fi to this site.
05 · NOTIFYThe caregiver gets a WhatsApp message with a short summary and a map link.

Alert rules

  • Fall: impact above the threshold, then stillness, then a 10-second countdown; one press cancels.
  • Manual fall: press the button on the band.
  • Low oxygen: SpO₂ below 90% for 10 seconds.
  • Heart rate warning: below 50 or above 120 (display only, no alert sent).
